PART 1: BUSINESS STRATEGY
1. Executive Proposal & The Unique Opportunity

Modern enterprises waste billions annually on "Meeting Amnesia." Highly paid cross-functional leaders make critical commitments in planning and architecture syncs, only for those tasks to evaporate into unread Slack threads, fragmented documents, or forgotten boards. Traditional project management software fails due to extreme behavioral friction (forcing humans to fill out 10 fields per ticket post-meeting). Meanwhile, standard AI note-takers fail because they generate passive 5-page transcript dumps that nobody reads.

The MeetingPilot Breakthrough: We separate cognitive synthesis from deterministic storage via the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Claude serves as the reasoning brain (parsing conversational context, identifying commitments, and structuring decisions), while MeetingPilot serves as a Zero AI Inside deterministic database and real-time dashboard. Customers bring their own Claude subscriptions (BYO-AI), yielding software-grade 97.5%+ gross margins with zero hallucination risk.
2. Market Problem & Competitive Gaps
01. Post-Meeting Friction
  • Teams spend 35%+ of time in syncs.
  • Post-call ticket creation takes 15–20 minutes, leading to abandoned tasks.
  • 70% of verbal commitments slip past deadlines without assigned owners.
02. Flaw of AI Note-Takers
  • Tools like Otter and Fireflies dump 5-page transcripts executives ignore.
  • Passive summaries do NOT equal operational execution or accountability.
  • Agreed decisions are buried, forcing teams to re-litigate settled topics.
03. The AI Wrapper Trap
  • Standard wrappers pay $0.05–$0.20 per query in OpenAI/Anthropic bills.
  • Gross margins get crushed to 35-50% as user volume scales.
  • Direct LLM writes introduce severe hallucination risk into corporate DBs.

2. Claude & Model Context Protocol (MCP) Integration Architecture

The system connects Claude as the external cognitive layer. Claude ingests messy conversational transcripts and automatically chooses among 15 registered tools:

Functional ClusterAvailable MCP ToolsOperational Purpose & Architecture
Meeting Lifecycle create_meeting, list_meetings, get_meeting, update_meeting, delete_meeting Creates meetings with attendees, agenda, tags, and date; queries past context without leaving chat.
Action Management add_action_item, list_action_items, update_action_item, delete_action_item Extracts commitments with explicit owner, deadline, and priority (Low, Medium, High, Urgent).
Decision Register record_meeting_decision, list_meeting_decisions Preserves organizational rationale and strategic consensus independent of operational task tickets.
Velocity & Governance get_dashboard_summary, get_team_analytics, create_spend_approval, approve_action Calculates team Say-Do execution ratio and owner breakdowns; tracks meeting-related financial spend.
